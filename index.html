Hermes Workspace Automation

Hermes Workspace Automation is a private productivity tool used by its owner to automate authorized tasks in Google Drive and Google Sheets.

Private use: This application is not offered as a public service and does not provide public account registration.

What the application does

  • Creates, reads, and updates Google Sheets requested by the owner.
  • Organizes files within Google Drive locations authorized by the owner.
  • Uses Google OAuth so the owner can grant and revoke access.
